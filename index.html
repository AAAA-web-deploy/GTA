Meet the crew.

When the sun fades and the neon glows, a Jedi, a senator and a few determined outlaws make an unlikely crew.

AnakinSkywalker

The Force behind the wheel.

A gifted pilot with a lightsaber, a quick temper and a knack for finding the fastest way out.

PadméAmidala

The strategist.

Sharp, composed and impossible to intimidate, Padmé keeps the crew focused when every deal starts to unravel.

Jason Duval

The local driver.

Jason knows Leonida’s back roads and the difference between a clean getaway and an expensive mistake.

Lucia Caminos

The risk taker.

Ambitious and resourceful, Lucia sees opportunity where everyone else sees a dead end.

Han Solo

The smuggler.

If the score needs a fast ship or a questionable connection, Han probably knows someone.

Lando Calrissian

The dealmaker.

A smooth operator with a plan, a sharp suit and a talent for getting into exclusive places.

Ahsoka Tano

The independent.

A formidable ally who follows her own judgment when the job turns complicated.

C-3PO

The translator.

Fluent in millions of forms of communication—and increasingly concerned about the crew’s plans.

Get in / How to buy

How to buy.

If a token is launched, you will need an Ethereum wallet, ETH for network fees and a trusted decentralized exchange. Verify the token contract before any swap.

  1. 01

    Get a wallet

    Install MetaMask or another Ethereum wallet, then create or import your account.

  2. 02

    Add ETH

    Buy ETH on an exchange and send it to your wallet. You need ETH for the swap and gas.

  3. 03

    Open Uniswap

    Go to Uniswap on Ethereum, connect your wallet, and set the network to Ethereum mainnet.

  4. 04

    Check the token

    When the official address is available, verify it and the token details before swapping. Never share your recovery phrase.
